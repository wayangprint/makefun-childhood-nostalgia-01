A tiny Node program that writes one original sentence recalling a childhood memory — a grandmother's kitchen, rice just steamed, rain beginning, the warm smell fogging a child's glasses — to /output/entry.txt for make.fun's Make the AI Judge Feel Nostalgic: A Childhood Memory.

> In my grandmother's kitchen, the rice had just finished steaming when the rain began, and she lifted the lid so the warm smell fogged my glasses while she spooned it onto my plate like feeding me was still the most important job in the world.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
